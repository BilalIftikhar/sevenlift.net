# Seven Lift: Backlink and Outreach Plan

Who this is for: whoever runs Seven Lift's SEO outreach. Use it alongside
`docs/business-listing-kit.md`, which holds the exact business details,
directory list, review requests and the "do not" rules. This file covers
earning links from other websites, which is the main lever left for ranking
once the site's pages are in good shape.

Copy the business name, phone and website exactly as in the listing kit
every time.

---

## 1. What we can offer other sites

Sites link to pages that are useful to their own readers, not to service
pages. These pages exist for that purpose:

| Page | Why someone would link to it | Who to offer it to |
|---|---|---|
| `/tools/forklift-capacity-calculator` | Free tool for counterbalance forklifts, using the figures on any data plate | Warehouse and logistics blogs, training institutes, HSE resource pages |
| `/blog/how-to-read-crane-load-chart` | Clear explanation with a worked example | Rigging and lifting training providers, engineering student groups |
| `/blog/forklift-daily-inspection-checklist` | Ready-to-copy checklist | HSE consultants, facility managers, warehouse operators |

## 2. Outreach targets, in priority order

### a. People who already know us (easiest links)

- **Customers.** After each job, ask for a mention on their projects or
  suppliers page (template in the listing kit). Offer job photos they can use.
- **Suppliers and partners.** Inspection companies, transport firms, parts
  suppliers. Ask to be listed on their customers or partners page.
- **Subcontractors we hire.** Riggers, operators' training providers,
  transport companies.

Aim: 2 to 4 of these a month. They are the most relevant links we can get.

### b. Industry media (expert comment)

UAE construction and logistics publications such as Construction Week
Middle East, Big Project ME, Logistics Middle East and MEP Middle East, plus
the business sections of Gulf News, Khaleej Times and The National.

- Pitch short expert comments tied to the season: summer midday break
  (June), shamal winds, Ramadan working hours, big project announcements.
- Offer a 600 to 800 word article adapted from one of our guides (template
  in the listing kit). Never send the same text that is on our site; rewrite
  it for their readers.
- Sign up to journalist-request services and answer questions on cranes,
  equipment rental, construction safety and logistics in the UAE.

### c. Associations and chambers

- Abu Dhabi Chamber and Dubai Chamber member directories, if Seven Lift is a
  member.
- Lifting and safety associations with UAE chapters, if and when Seven Lift
  joins one. Only claim memberships that are real.

### d. Resource pages

Search Google for pages that already list useful links on our topics, then
ask to be added:

- `forklift safety resources UAE`
- `"useful links" crane safety`
- `warehouse safety checklist resources`
- `lifting operations resources site:ae`

Offer the specific page that fits, never the homepage.

### e. Exhibition and event companies

Stand builders and event production firms that work at ADNEC, Dubai World
Trade Centre and Expo City often keep supplier lists. Offer the exhibition man
lift guide and a supplier listing.

## 3. Unlinked mentions

Once a month, search Google for `"Seven Lift" -site:sevenlift.net` and
`"sevenlift" -site:sevenlift.net`. If a page mentions Seven Lift without a
link, email the author and ask politely for the mention to link to
`https://www.sevenlift.net`.

## 5. Tracking

Keep a simple sheet with these columns: date, site, contact, page offered,
status (sent, replied, linked, declined), link URL, follow-up date.

Follow up once, after about a week. Do not chase more than that.

Monthly targets:

| Activity | Target |
|---|---|
| Customer or supplier links requested | 4 |
| Media pitches or journalist-request answers | 4 |
| Resource page outreach emails | 10 |
| Unlinked mention check | 1 |
| New Google reviews (see listing kit) | 5 |

## 6. Never

Everything in section 6 of the listing kit applies: no bought links, no link
exchanges, no private blog networks, no paid "guest post" packages. One
relevant link from a real UAE company is worth more than a hundred of those,
and the bad ones can get the site penalised.
